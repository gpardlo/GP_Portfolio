About Me

Gabriella Pardlo is an undergraduate researcher, Honors Economics major, and Mathematics minor from Brooklyn, New York by way of Abu Dhabi, UAE. Her research interests center the economics of education, with a focus on how educational policy and investment shape long-term outcomes for underserved communities. She brings her work as a 2026 Fellow in the American Economic Association Summer Training Program to her current research project, exploring whether Opportunity Zone designations have measurable impacts on public education outcomes in New York City. Outside of research, she is the founding president of the Howard University intramural swim club.
